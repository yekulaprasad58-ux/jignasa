Jignasa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · App: Jignasa (com.jignasa.app)

Summary

  • No account, no login, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your learning progress, settings and chat history are stored only on your phone.
  • To create questions and answers, request text is sent to Google's Gemini API.
  • We never sell or share your personal data.

Data the app handles

  • On your phone only: cards you learned, reactions, streak, notification settings, chosen language and your chat history in the "Ask" tab. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app (it may be included in your phone's own Google backup, if you have turned that on).
  • Sent to Google Gemini API: the instructions needed to create content — for example the subjects to cover, recent question topics, the language, and anything you type in "Ask" or follow-up questions. Please do not type personal information there. Google processes this data under the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Reports: if you tap "Report" on AI content, the reported text may be sent to the developer so it can be reviewed. No personal details are attached.

Notifications

Curiosity notifications are scheduled on your phone. You can pause them in the app's Settings or turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

Jignasa is intended for learners aged 13 and above. It is not designed for children under 13.

Security

Data sent to the Gemini API travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
